Quick Start Overview

Catching up on hundreds of messages takes under 30 seconds across three simple steps.

1

Export or Copy Chat

Export your conversation from WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, or Facebook Messenger, or simply copy timestamped or Name: message lines directly.

2

STEP-BY-STEP EXPORT INSTRUCTIONS

Messages Import Guide

What Did I Miss? does not connect directly to any messaging account (for your safety and security). Instead, you import exported chat files or paste messages. Select your platform below for exact export instructions.

💬

WhatsApp Export & Import

Supported file types: .txt, .zip, or pasted text transcripts

Supported Formats: .txt .zip
Timestamp Styles: iOS brackets [DD/MM/YY, HH:MM] & Android dashes DD/MM/YY, HH:MM -

On iPhone (iOS):

1
Open WhatsApp and select your chat or group

Tap on the contact name or group subject at the very top of the conversation screen.

2
Tap "Export Chat"

Scroll down to the bottom of the Contact/Group Info screen and select Export Chat.

3
Choose "Without Media"

Always select Without Media. This keeps the file lightweight, avoids large video/photo payloads, and generates an instant _chat.txt or .zip.

4
Save to Files

In the iOS Share sheet, tap Save to Files and choose your Downloads or On My iPhone folder.

5
Import into What Did I Miss?

Open What Did I Miss?, tap Import Conversations > Choose Export Files, and pick your saved file.

On Android:

1
Open WhatsApp chat and tap the three dots (⋮)

Tap the menu icon in the upper-right corner of the chat window.

2
Select "More" > "Export chat"

Choose Without media when prompted.

3
Save file to device

Save to your device's Files or Downloads folder (or email it to yourself/Google Drive to download on desktop).

💡
Fast Alternative (Direct Copy & Paste): You can select recent messages directly in WhatsApp Web or WhatsApp Desktop, press ⌘C / Ctrl+C, click Paste Messages in What Did I Miss?, and paste them right in!
✈️

Telegram Export & Import

Supported file types: .json (Telegram Desktop), .html, or copied text

Recommended Export: Telegram Desktop JSON export (result.json)
Also Supported: messages.html, ISO timestamp text

Using Telegram Desktop (Best Quality Export):

1
Open Telegram Desktop and open your chat

Works for private chats, group conversations, and supergroups.

2
Click the three dots (⋮) > "Export chat history"

In the upper right corner of the chat header, click the menu button and choose Export chat history.

3
Configure export settings

Uncheck photos, videos, voice messages, and stickers. Under Format, select Machine-readable JSON (or HTML).

4
Click "Export" and choose file in What Did I Miss?

Open What Did I Miss?, choose your exported result.json or messages.html, and receive an instant breakdown!

ℹ️
Multiline Message Preservation: Telegram messages often have multiple lines, code blocks, or bullets. What Did I Miss? automatically groups multiline messages under the original sender without fragmenting.
📸

Instagram Direct Messages Export & Import

Supported file types: message_1.json, multi-part split JSON files, or full ZIP

Format Requirement: Select JSON format (not HTML) in Meta Accounts Center
Automatic Merging: Split files (message_1.json, message_2.json) are auto-combined

How to Request an Instagram Message Export:

1
Open Instagram Settings

Go to your Instagram profile > menu (☰) > Your Activity > Download your information (or Meta Accounts Center > Your information and permissions).

2
Request a Download for Messages

Select Some of your information, then check only Messages.

3
Select Format: JSON

Important: Set Format to JSON. Set media quality to Low (since media isn't needed) and choose your date range.

4
Download ZIP and Select in What Did I Miss?

Meta sends you an email when your file is ready. Download it, select the ZIP or the specific message_1.json from the chat folder in the app.

💡
Split Folder Multi-File Support: When an Instagram DM conversation is long, Meta splits it across message_1.json, message_2.json, etc. You can select all files at once — What Did I Miss? detects they belong to the same chat and merges them chronologically!
⚡

Facebook Messenger Export & Import

Supported file types: Messenger export .json or compressed .zip

Format Requirement: Choose JSON format in Meta Accounts Center
Detection: Automatically resolves conversation title and participant names

How to Export from Facebook / Messenger:

1
Navigate to Meta Accounts Center

On Facebook or Messenger, click your profile picture > Settings & Privacy > Settings > Accounts Center.

2
Download Your Information

Click Your information and permissions > Download your information > Download or transfer information.

3
Select Messages in JSON Format

Select Messages only. Choose JSON format and submit request.

4
Import into What Did I Miss?

Import the downloaded chat folder or message_1.json file. What Did I Miss? decodes the timestamps and participants immediately.

📄

CSV, Slack, Discord & Custom Transcripts

Supported file types: .csv, .txt, or any raw copied text

CSV Headers Recognized: sender, timestamp, message (or text, content, author)
Plain Text: Standard Name: message format supported

Using CSV Files:

You can export chats from Slack, Discord, internal ticketing systems, or spreadsheets into a CSV file. What Did I Miss? automatically maps common column names:

Recognized Field Accepted Header Column Names Example Value
Sender sender, author, from, username, sendername "Sarah Connor"
Message Text (Required) message, content, text, body "Please review the PR by tomorrow afternoon."
Timestamp (Optional) timestamp, datetime, date, createdat, time "2026-11-19T10:30:00Z"

Plain Text "Name: message" Transcripts:

Even without dates or timestamps, simply pasting lines structured as Name: message is fully parsed. Multiline continuations without a new colon are cleanly attached to the previous speaker.

App Features & Findings Categories

What Did I Miss? uses transparent, deterministic heuristics to rank messages by urgency and signal rather than slow, unpredictable black-box cloud AI models. Here are the 5 core categories identified:

✓ Action Item

1. Directed Action Items & Commitments

Identifies explicit tasks directed to team members, commitments made by speakers, and actionable requests. Includes interactive checkboxes to check off tasks as you finish them.

Trigger cues: please review, can you send, I will deploy, we plan to update, need you to
◆ Decision & Update

2. Key Decisions & Announcements

Captures team consensus, official decisions, and major status updates so you know what was decided without reading 80 back-and-forth messages.

Trigger cues: we agreed on, decided to, approved, heads up, is live, incident
⏰ Deadline & Urgency

3. Deadlines & Urgent Blockers

Extracts both explicit dates ("by Friday 5pm", "due Nov 30") and relative time cues ("due tomorrow", "tonight"). Differentiates true urgency from routine mentions.

Trigger cues: due by, deadline is, before Monday, urgent, critical blocker, ASAP
@ Mention

4. Personalized Mentions & Highlights

Tracks standard @username mentions. You can also specify custom keywords (like your first name, nicknames, or project code names) in the import modal to highlight messages relevant to you.

Customizable: Specify comma-separated keywords in "Your name or mention keywords"

  • Interactive Task Checklist: Mark action items as completed directly in the summary panel; status updates in real-time.
  • Finding Dismissal: Dismiss items you have already handled or that don't apply to you with one click.
  • Multi-Conversation Workspace: Import multiple threads at once; switch between them seamlessly.
  • Overview Metric Strip: Instant summary of total conversations, unread count, actions identified, and direct mentions.
  • Thread Color Dots: Visual color accents (Purple, Coral, Blue, Green) to distinguish channels and conversations at a glance.

SECURITY GUARANTEES

Privacy & Local Processing Architecture

Your conversations belong to you alone. Learn how What Did I Miss? ensures 100% privacy.

🛡️
Zero Data Leaves Your Device: All parsing, indexing, and analysis takes place in memory in this browser tab. There are no tracking scripts, no third-party analytics, and no remote server uploads.

1. In-Memory Session Storage

Imported messages are held strictly in temporary JavaScript RAM variables for the current session. Nothing is written to localStorage, IndexedDB, or tracking cookies. Reloading or closing the tab instantly vaporizes all data.

2. No External AI API Requests

Unlike cloud-based summarizing apps, What Did I Miss? does not send your private messages to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any remote AI server. Everything runs on client-side pattern matching algorithms.

3. Verifiable Network Isolation

You can verify this yourself: open your browser's Developer Tools (F12), switch to the Network tab, import a chat, and observe: zero outgoing HTTP or WebSocket requests occur.

4. Offline Operability

Once the static files are loaded, you can turn off Wi-Fi or turn on Airplane mode. The application remains 100% functional without internet connectivity.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about formats, security, and troubleshooting.

Why does What Did I Miss? say "Ambiguous date order"?

When an export uses a numerical date where both numbers are 12 or less (for example, 04/05/26), it is impossible to know with 100% certainty whether it represents April 5th (US format) or May 4th (International format) without guessing.

Rather than making an incorrect guess, What Did I Miss? retains the exact raw timestamp so the chronological sequence remains honest and trustworthy.

Why did my ZIP file fail on a mobile browser?

In-memory ZIP file decompression requires the browser's built-in DecompressionStream("deflate-raw") API. While modern desktop browsers and recent mobile versions support it, some older mobile browsers do not.

Easy Fix: Simply tap the ZIP file in your phone's native Files or Downloads app to extract it, and select the resulting .txt or .json file in What Did I Miss?.

Can the app connect directly to my WhatsApp or Telegram account?

No, by design. Direct connections would require you to share session credentials, API keys, or phone numbers, which introduces severe security risks.

By requiring exported files or copied text, you maintain absolute control over what is analyzed, ensuring no unauthorized account access is ever possible.

Can I export my action items and summary?

Yes! You can easily select and copy any action item, decision, or summary paragraph directly from the detail panel to paste into your notes, Notion, Trello, or email.

How do I completely erase all conversation data?

Click the Clear Data (⌫) button in the top navigation bar, or simply refresh or close your browser tab. Because data is never saved to disk or database, closing the page instantly purges everything.
